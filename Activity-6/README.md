## ✨ Description

For this activity, we were tasked to create a one-page website based on a personal portfolio, business idea, or advocacy project. As a fourth-year nursing student currently focused on ER topics and duties, I came up with **ERSim (Emergency Nursing Simulation Platform)**, an AI-assisted website concept designed to help nursing students practice emergency scenarios and enhance their clinical decision-making skills.

---

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

- 💜 I learned how to use AI tools such as ChatGPT and Base44 to plan and develop a nursing-related website.
- 🎨 I gained knowledge in website design, user interface, and organizing features to create an engaging and user-friendly platform.
- 🌷 One challenge I faced was using Replit AI because some of its features required a subscription. I solved this by switching to Base44 as an alternative platform.
- ✨ I learned the importance of flexibility, creativity, and resourcefulness when developing digital projects.
- 🚀 In the future, I can apply these skills to create innovative educational platforms and improve my knowledge of digital technology in nursing.
