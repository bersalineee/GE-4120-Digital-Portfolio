# Laboratory Midterm Examination

## About the Activity
This activity is our Laboratory Midterm Examination, where our professor tests our practical skills by giving us instructions and asking us to complete the required tasks within **10 minutes**.

We are provided with specific data and details that we need to process using Google Workspace applications. The activity assesses our ability to work efficiently, follow instructions, compute data accurately, and organize our outputs.

## Google Workspace Applications Used

### 1. Google Sheets
We first use Google Sheets to input the given data, perform the necessary computations, and obtain the results.

### 2. Google Docs
After completing the computations in Google Sheets, we use Google Docs to present and organize the results in a proper document format.

### 3. Google Drive
Finally, we upload and store our completed outputs in Google Drive for submission.
