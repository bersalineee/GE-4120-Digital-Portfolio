## ✨ Description

In this activity, I created a **personal branding kit** consisting of a personal logo, tagline, color palette, typography set, and resume header. I carefully selected colors and fonts that reflect my personality and create a consistent, readable, and professional visual identity.

---

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

- 💜 I learned how to create a personal branding kit that represents my identity and personality.
- 🎨 I gained knowledge in selecting suitable color palettes and typography to establish a consistent visual identity.
- 🌷 One challenge I faced was choosing colors and fonts that complement each other while maintaining readability. I addressed this by carefully selecting and combining appropriate design elements.
- ✨ I learned the importance of consistency in personal branding, especially when designing logos, taglines, and professional materials.
- 🚀 In the future, I can apply these skills to improve my digital portfolio, resume, presentations, and other professional designs.
