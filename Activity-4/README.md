## 💡 What Have I Learned?

- 💜 I learned about the different functions, features, and formulas of Google Sheets and how they can be used to organize and manage data efficiently.
- 🎨 I gained knowledge in using spreadsheets for calculations, data analysis, and presenting information in an organized format.
- 🌷 One challenge we faced was understanding the different formulas and organizing the information for our class presentation. We addressed this by reviewing their functions, practicing how to use them, and collaborating with our groupmates to ensure that the topics were clearly explained.
