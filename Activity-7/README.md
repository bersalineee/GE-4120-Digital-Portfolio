## ✨ Description

For this activity, we were tasked to create a one-page professional resume using at least two Large Language Model (LLM) tools with the same prompt, then analyze and compare their outputs. I used ChatGPT and Claude AI to generate my nursing resume and evaluated their outputs based on structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality.

---

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

- 💜 I learned how to use different AI tools, such as ChatGPT and Claude AI, to generate a professional resume using the same prompt.
- 🎨 I discovered that different LLMs can produce different results in terms of design, structure, and presentation, even when given identical instructions.
- 🌷 One challenge I faced was determining which resume output was more suitable for a professional nursing application. I addressed this by comparing both outputs based on readability, organization, and overall quality.
- ✨ I learned the importance of writing clear and specific prompts to achieve accurate and well-organized AI-generated outputs.
- 🚀 In the future, I can apply these skills to create professional documents, improve my resume, and use AI tools responsibly in my academic and professional career.
